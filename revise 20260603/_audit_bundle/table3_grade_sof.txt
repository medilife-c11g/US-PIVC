Table 3. GRADE Summary of Findings — post-insertion outcomes of ultrasound-guided versus landmark peripheral intravenous catheters
RR > 1 indicates a higher event rate (worse outcome) with ultrasound guidance. GRADE certainty: ⊕⊕⊕⊕ High, ⊕⊕⊕◯ Moderate, ⊕⊕◯◯ Low, ⊕◯◯◯ Very low. Certainty was rated per outcome using the GRADE approach (risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision, publication bias); observational cohorts started at low certainty. Publication bias could not be formally assessed (fewer than 10 studies per outcome).
