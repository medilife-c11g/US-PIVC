# Supplementary Material S2: List of Studies Excluded at Full-Text Stage with Reasons

## Studies excluded after full-text review (n = 4)

*(Revised 2026-06-03 to align with the corrected PRISMA flow diagram and manuscript: the Avelar trial is now included via its English 2015 report, so its 2013 Portuguese record is re-stated as a non-English companion; and the two records previously listed here that were in fact single-reviewer title/abstract includes adjudicated out at the title/abstract stage — the ICU-nurses registration and the Kleidon J Vasc Access 2022 abstract — have been moved out of the full-text-exclusion table, bringing it from 6 to 4. 19 reports assessed − 4 excluded = 15 included.)*

| # | Study | Reason for Exclusion | Detail |

| 1 | Malik A et al. (2023) "Outcomes of Ultrasound Guided Peripheral Intravenous Catheters Placed in the Emergency Department and Factors Associated with Survival." *Open Access Emergency Medicine*. | Single-arm study, no comparator | Descriptive study of 388 USIV placements only; no landmark/palpation comparison group. | 3 | Unpublished manuscript — "Effectiveness of Ultrasound-Guided Versus Traditional IV Insertion by Oncology Nurses in Adult Population Receiving Systemic Anti-cancer Therapy" (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07208175). | Unpublished | Trial registration only; no published results at time of search. (Rayyan-486160685) |
| 4 | Kleidon TM et al. (2023) "Ultrasound-guided PIVC insertion: a randomised controlled trial protocol." *British Journal of Nursing*. | Overlapping report of an included trial | Protocol-only publication of the EPIC trial — the same trial as the included Kleidon 2025 primary report (*JAMA Pediatrics*). No post-insertion outcome data reported. (Rayyan-486160240) |

**Note (title/abstract-stage exclusions, not full-text exclusions):** Two records initially flagged by a single reviewer — the ICU-nurses trial registration (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03745209; Rayyan-486160677) and the Kleidon et al. conference abstract (*Journal of Vascular Access*, 2022; Rayyan-486160691, an overlapping report of the EPIC trial) — were adjudicated out at the title/abstract screening stage and are therefore counted among the 1,340 title/abstract exclusions, not the four full-text exclusions above.
